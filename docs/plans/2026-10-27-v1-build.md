## Rules

- Brand tokens only. No raw hex, spacing or radii in components; Tailwind 4 `@theme` maps to the CSS variables.
- Light and dark from the same variables: `prefers-color-scheme` by default, overridden by the theme toggle (`data-theme` on `<html>`), remembered.
- Fonts: Newsreader (display, headings), Public Sans (text), JetBrains Mono (numbers, labels, code), self-hosted, subset, preloaded for the headline. No Google Fonts `@import`.
- One `display-xl` per page; mango once per view (the monogram cursor is the exception); real minus sign; focus ring 2px mango with 2px offset.
- Performance contract (hero brief): headline is the LCP element, poster SVG before 3D, no raster in the hero, reduced-motion = final grown state. Targets: Lighthouse 95+ mobile, 100 a11y/best practices/SEO, CLS 0.
- en / pt / es for every string; check wrapping at 390 in Portuguese.
- Keep `/projects/:slug` URLs. All Sanity fetches stay in `createServerFn`.
- Tests: Vitest + Testing Library as in `src/test/`. `NODE_OPTIONS=--no-experimental-webstorage` on Node 26. `pnpm` is not on PATH: use `npx -y pnpm@10`.
